Accessibility-first UI

Adaptive UI Modality Engine

An interface that notices how you are interacting and reshapes itself: bigger targets for fingers, strong focus rings for keyboards, spoken control for hands-free use and dwell-to-click for eye control.

Mouse

Fine pointer: compact layout with hover effects.

Say "help" to hear the commands.

Hover over a button to see the hover highlight (mouse only).

Keyboard mode: use Tab and Shift+Tab to move, Space or Enter to activate. The orange ring shows where you are.

Playground: one gallery, every input

Click the buttons, swipe the card, press ← →, say "next", "previous" or "like", or dwell your gaze on a button.

Gesture pad

✋
Swipe, pinch, double tap or press and hold here
  • ← → switch input mode
  • ↑ ↓ or pinch: text size
  • Double tap: toggle theme
  • Hold: microphone on/off

Eye control and camera

Eye keypad: look at a tile until the ring fills and it will speak the phrase.

The camera uses motion, not pupil tracking: wave left or right to switch modes. In Eye mode, moving your head steers the pointer.
